# Tutorials

The tutorials form a progressive course. The first five cover the normal analysis loop; the remaining chapters develop production-scale and physics-specific techniques.

<div class="tutorial-grid">
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>1. Data I/O</strong><p>Build datasets, stream ROOT/Parquet, select events, and write results.</p></div>
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>2. Expressions</strong><p>Compose scalars, vectors, four-vectors, and symbolic linear algebra.</p></div>
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>3. Quantum numbers</strong><p>Enumerate projections and angular-momentum couplings exactly.</p></div>
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>4. Phase-space MC</strong><p>Describe generation proposals and validate kinematics.</p></div>
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>5. Modeled MC</strong><p>Weight or unweight proposals with a physical intensity.</p></div>
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>6. Fits</strong><p>Normalize with accepted MC, optimize, diagnose, and project.</p></div>
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>7. Advanced models</strong><p>Construct a polarized photoproduction intensity explicitly.</p></div>
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>8. Simultaneous fits</strong><p>Combine efficiencies, extended yields, and samples with different exposures.</p></div>
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>9. Coupled channels</strong><p>Share resonance parameters across production channels.</p></div>
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>10. Selection rules</strong><p>Evaluate conservation laws and enumerate allowed partial waves.</p></div>
  <div class="tutorial-card"><strong>11. Cross sections</strong><p>Propagate fit ensembles, combine exposures, and produce differential results.</p></div>
</div>
